Domain layer over Nocturne

Evidence you can see

A measurement, a population association, a curated classification, an estimate and a documented fact are five different kinds of knowing. This layer gives each one a form, so no view can render them alike. Nocturne supplies the ground, the type and the controls; everything on this page is what the science adds on top.

01 — Evidence kind

Five marks, one accent

The palette is mono, so the kinds separate by form, not hue: how the mark is filled, and how its edges end. Every value in the app carries its kind's mark within reading distance.

Measured
measured

A solid block with hard edges. The chip read this base at this position. Error rate exists but the bounds do not move.

Genotyping · Genotype Store
Association
statistical-association

A point inside a plume. The plume is the study population; the point is not the reader. Never rendered as a statement about the person.

Statistical genetics · GWAS pack
Classification
curated-classification

An outline around a judgement. A panel decided this, under review criteria, on a date. It can be revised, so it is never filled solid.

Clinical genetics · ClinVar pack
Estimate
probabilistic-estimate

A band that fades out at both ends, with a tick at the likeliest value. The fade is the point: the bounds are not known.

Genetic genealogy · Inheritance Analysis
Documentary
documentary

Ruled lines, in the neutral ramp, never the accent. A record says so. Its weight comes from the source, which is always named.

Documentary genealogy · Pedigree

The five marks are drawn from one accent and one neutral ramp, so they survive on any ground and never read as a traffic-light scale. Nothing in this set encodes good or bad.
02 — Uncertainty

Edges carry certainty

Nocturne fades its rules to transparent at the ends. Here that becomes a rule of meaning: a hard end is a known bound, a faded end is an unknown one. One habit covers cM ranges, frequencies, segment boundaries and confidence intervals, and it needs no extra colour.

Ranges are drawn, never rounded to a single answer. A relationship estimate always shows its overlapping candidates.

No-call

Hatched, never blank and never zero. The chip returned nothing here; the absence is data.

Strand-ambiguous

A double outline on an A/T or C/G call. Measured, but the strand can't be inferred, so joins on it are flagged.

Proposed

A dashed edge marks anything awaiting the human gate — an estimate offered to the pedigree, not yet confirmed.

Stale build

Anything whose build or pack version no longer matches the kit drops to the neutral ramp and states the mismatch.

06 — Writing

What the interface may say

Instead of “you have a variant”
Differs from the GRCh37 reference

The reference allele is the one in the assembly, not the healthy one.

Instead of “clinical significance”
ClinVar classification

“Significance” means a p-value to one science and a pathogenicity class to another. Use neither loosely.

Instead of “23% Scandinavian”
Similarity to reference panels

Panel similarity and named ancestors stay separate concepts, in separate contexts.

Instead of “your risk is”
This study reported an odds ratio of…

The app shows what sources say, with citations. It computes no personal risk.
